Time Clock – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Time Clock is a small web app for recording work hours. This page explains what happens to your data.

What the app stores

The hours, notes and settings you enter are stored in your own browser on your own device. The app has no server of its own and the developer cannot see this data.

Optional Google sign-in and sync

If you choose "Sign in with Google", the app asks for these permissions:

  • Google Drive – app data folder (drive.appdata): lets the app keep one small file with your hours in a hidden folder inside your own Google Drive. Only this app can open that folder. The app cannot see, read or change any other file in your Drive.
  • Google Drive – files created by this app (drive.file): lets the app create and update one spreadsheet named "Time Clock – backup" and a folder "Time Clock – reports" (one hours report per month in the company template) in your own Google Drive. They hold a readable copy of your shifts and day notes, so your hours are safe even if the app or a device fails. The app can only open files it created itself; it cannot see, read or change any other file in your Drive. You can turn this backup off in Settings.
  • Email address (userinfo.email): used only to show which Google account is signed in on the screen.

Your hours are sent only between your device and your own Google Drive. They are never sent to the developer or to any other party.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, store, sell, share or analyse your personal data.
  • We do not use advertising, tracking or analytics.
  • Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

  • In the app: Settings → "Sign out & clear this device" removes the data from the device.
  • In Google: Google Account → Data & privacy → Third-party apps and services → Time Clock → "Delete app data" or "Delete all connections". This removes the hidden app file from your Drive and revokes the app's access.
  • The backup spreadsheet "Time Clock – backup" and the folder "Time Clock – reports" are ordinary files in your Drive: delete them there like any other file.
